Orders = contracts traded in the latest session with an inferred side (calls near ask / puts near bid = bullish; puts near ask / calls near bid = bearish). Inferred from delayed public chain data; not confirmed sweeps or blocks. Mid-priced trades are excluded. Click a row to filter the flow table.
Data is derived from delayed (~15 min) public option-chain snapshots (Yahoo Finance via yfinance), refreshed about every 30 min in market hours — not a live trade-by-trade tape. Sweeps, blocks, and true buy/sell side can't be confirmed. "Side" and sentiment are inferred from last price vs. bid/ask (near ask → call bullish / put bearish; near bid → the reverse). Row time = contract's last trade time. Contracts whose last trade is not in the latest session are excluded from volume/premium (Yahoo can carry stale volume).
